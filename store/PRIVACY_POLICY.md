## Who we are
SkinScript ("the app", "we") is a dermatology board-exam study tool provided by
**Dr. Osama Al Rawi** ("we", "us"). Contact: **[support email]** · https://osamah.co

## Who the app is for
SkinScript is an invite-only study tool for dermatology residents, doctors and
board candidates. It is **not intended for children** and is not directed at
anyone under 18.

## What we collect
We only collect what the app needs to work. We do **not** use advertising,
cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics.

- **Account information** — your email address, and/or mobile phone number if you
  sign in by SMS; a username and display name; and a unique account identifier.
- **Study data** — your quiz answers, progress, exam history, flagged and
  "incorrect" questions, spaced-repetition schedule, notes, highlights,
  flashcards and any feedback you send. This is tied to your account so it can
  sync across your devices.
- **Access code** — the invite code you use to register.
- **Technical information** — if you enable study reminders on the web, we store
  the browser push subscription and user-agent string so reminders can be sent.

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, health records, financial
information, or browsing history.

## How we use your data
- To create and secure your account and sign you in.
- To save and sync your study progress across your devices.
- To operate app features (leaderboard opt-in, reminders, feedback to the app owner).
- To respond to problems or feedback you report.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Service providers (processors)
We use trusted providers to run the app. They process data only to provide their
service to us:
- **Supabase** — hosting, authentication, database and image storage.
- **[Twilio / your SMS provider]** — sending one-time SMS sign-in codes (only if you use SMS login).
- **[Email provider]** — sending account and password-reset emails.

## Where data is stored
Your data is stored on our Supabase project's servers in **[region]**. By using
the app you understand your data may be processed there.

## Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your records is restricted by
row-level security so each account can only read its own data.

## Keeping and deleting your data
- You can clear your study data at any time with **"Reset all progress"** in the app.
- You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing
  **[support email]**; we will delete it within **[30] days**.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy; we'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for
significant changes, note it in the app.
