Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

SkinScript (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a dermatology board-exam study tool provided by Dr. Osama Al Rawi. You can reach us at usamaayman@gmail.com or osamah.co.

Who the app is for

SkinScript is an invite-only study tool for dermatology residents, doctors and board candidates. It is not intended for children and is not directed at anyone under 18.

What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. We do not use advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics.

  • Account information — your email address, and/or mobile phone number if you sign in by SMS; a username and display name; and a unique account identifier.
  • Study data — your quiz answers, progress, exam history, flagged and “incorrect” questions, spaced-repetition schedule, notes, highlights, flashcards and any feedback you send. This is tied to your account so it can sync across your devices.
  • Access code — the invite code you use to register.
  • Technical information — if you enable study reminders in the web version, we store the browser push subscription and user-agent string so reminders can be sent.

We do not collect your location, contacts, health records, financial information, or browsing history.

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To save and sync your study progress across your devices.
  • To operate app features (optional leaderboard, reminders, and feedback to the app owner).
  • To respond to problems or feedback you report.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the app; they process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — hosting, authentication, database and image storage.
  • Our SMS and email delivery providers — sending one-time sign-in codes and account/password-reset emails.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider and may be processed in countries other than your own. By using the app you understand and agree to this processing.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your records is restricted by row-level security so each account can only read its own data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can clear your study data at any time with “Reset all progress” in the app.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing usamaayman@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

SkinScript is intended for medical professionals and trainees. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will change the “Last updated” date above and, for significant changes, note it in the app.

Contact

Questions about your data: usamaayman@gmail.com · osamah.co

SkinScript is a study tool for educational use only. It does not provide medical advice or clinical decision-making.
